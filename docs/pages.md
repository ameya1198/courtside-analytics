# Dashboard pages

Primary audience: a team's general manager or analytics staff.
Each page opens with its takeaway and uses 4 to 6 charts, summary first, detail last.

1. **League Pulse**: headline numbers with change vs last season, standings with net rating and last-10 trend, league 3-point rate by season, biggest movers.
2. **Team Report**: offensive vs defensive rating quadrants, rolling 10-game net rating, four factors vs league, shot zone mix, clutch and splits.
3. **Player Profile** (one page per player): percentile bars, game log with season band, zone shot chart, usage vs true shooting.
4. **Player Value**: salary vs production quadrants, age curve, top bargains and worst contracts (depends on a stable salary source).
5. **Rest and Schedule Impact**: rested vs back-to-back efficiency by team, schedule density heatmap.
6. **Matchup Scout**: two teams side by side, tendencies, shot zones, pace, clutch.
7. **Tonight**: live scores and top performers.

Rules: one highlight color, no pie charts or gauges, takeaway titles, works on mobile.
